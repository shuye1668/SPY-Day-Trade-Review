Treasury Secretary Bessent doubled long-bond buybacks on Aug 19, yet long-term yields kept climbing instead of falling—the intervention simply didn't work. Then Friday's S&P Global PMI hit a 52-month high, confirming the economy is running hot, which pushed yields even higher and cut the odds of rate cuts. Higher rates hurt what depends on cheap borrowing: utilities cratered 3–5% and high-priced tech lagged, while banks and cyclicals led the Dow to +0.98%. Bitcoin surged over 20% on the week. Underneath, investors punished heavy AI spenders like Alibaba. Next tests: Nvidia earnings and Fed Chair Warsh's Jackson Hole speech.
