Trump rejected Iran's plan to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, keeping oil near $105 and reviving inflation fears. Stronger-than-expected factory data added to the worry, pushing the 10-year Treasury yield to 5.23%, the highest since 2007, and lifting odds of an October Fed rate hike to about 70%. With safe bonds now paying more than stocks earn, investors sold stocks, bonds and even gold together; only the dollar rose. Within tech, buyers rewarded companies returning cash, like Nvidia with its record buyback, and dumped heavy AI spenders and borrowers such as AMD and Oracle. Stocks likely stay pressured until yields fall.
