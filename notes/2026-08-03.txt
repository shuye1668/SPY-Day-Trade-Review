Oil tumbled about 5% after President Trump called off a threatened strike on Iran and pointed to talks to reopen a key shipping route, easing the inflation fears that had dragged stocks down through July. Treasury yields fell, and a strong factory survey — its best in four years, yet with cooling prices — signaled growth without fresh inflation. Investors piled back into the big tech and AI names they had dumped just days earlier, giving them their best day since March; Amazon topped $3 trillion, and worries about Oracle's and SpaceX's debt eased. Chips and Apple lagged. The S&P closed near a record, its next move hinging on Friday's jobs data.
