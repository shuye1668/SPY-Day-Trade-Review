Fed Chair Warsh used his Jackson Hole debut to vow inflation would return to target, calling financial conditions un-restrictive and rates the "predominant tool" — reframing the path from when-to-cut toward whether-to-hike. Two-year yields leapt eleven basis points as hike odds cleared 50%, the dollar firmed and gold slipped, tightening conditions rather than sparking flight. Capital rotated out of long-duration growth: high-multiple semiconductors and small caps de-rated hardest, amplified by a Marvell/Nvidia post-earnings unwind. Liquidity migrated up the AI chain into cash-generative cloud names and rate-insensitive defensives, leaving indices flat but internals violently reshuffled. September payrolls now arbitrate.
