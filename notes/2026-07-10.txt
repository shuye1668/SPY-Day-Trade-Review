Meta jumped 6% after confirming its "Meta Compute" cloud business and Iris chip plans, and Nvidia rose 4% on eased UAE export rules — together lifting the S&P 500 (+0.4%) near record highs while SK Hynix soared 13% in its Nasdaq debut. Beneath the calm surface, money rotated hard: chipmakers, memory, and AI infrastructure won, while cybersecurity and software names (CrowdStrike, Datadog) fell 4–6% on fears AI undercuts their business models. Defensive REITs and staples also gained as the Fed stays hawkish. All eyes now turn to Tuesday's CPI, big-bank earnings, and Fed Chair Warsh's first testimony.
