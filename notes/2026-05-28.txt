Reports of a tentative 60-day US-Iran ceasefire sent oil tumbling, with Brent settling below $94. Cheaper oil eased inflation fears, so bond yields fell and the dollar weakened. With borrowing costs easing, money rushed back into beaten-down tech — and strong AI results from Snowflake and Dell added fuel, lifting the S&P 500, Nasdaq, and Dow to record highs. The rally was narrow, though: AI software and chips led while defensive stocks lagged. The catch — inflation stays sticky (core PCE near a three-year high) even as growth cools, so the Fed can't cut rates yet. If oil rebounds, this fragile advance could reverse.
