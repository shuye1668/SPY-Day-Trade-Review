June producer prices fell 0.3%, proving the Iran oil scare never pushed inflation higher. Traders slashed the odds of a July rate hike from 42% to 17%, and bond yields dropped. Cheaper borrowing costs sent money into rate-sensitive stocks: asset managers, real estate, and hospitals all rallied, with BlackRock jumping on record assets and CBRE climbing. Apple hit an all-time high after China approved its AI features. But memory chips crashed—SK Hynix cut its profit outlook and China's rising competition spooked investors, sending Micron and Western Digital down about 8%. The indexes looked calm, but underneath, the rotation was violent.
