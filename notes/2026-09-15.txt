The 10-year Treasury yield hit 5.04%, its highest since 2007, as Brent oil climbed to $109 and a weak 20-year bond auction showed buyers wanted higher returns to lend long-term. The S&P 500 fell 0.45% to 7,585.73 ahead of Wednesday's Fed decision, where a rate hike is nearly certain. Companies that borrow heavily to grow suffered most: Axon dropped 9.8% after announcing $1 billion in convertible bonds, and Oracle lost 3.1%. Big AI spenders Amazon and Microsoft fell. But chipmakers selling to them rose. AMD gained 2.2%, lifting the semiconductor index 0.4%. Investors await how many more hikes the Fed signals.
