Middle East ceasefire hopes drove SPX +0.7% and NDX +1.2% for a second straight session after Trump claimed Iran requested a truce. Memory/storage semiconductors led — WDC +10%, MU +8.9%, INTC +8.8% — as Brent crude fell 2.8% to ~$101. Goldman and JPMorgan trading desks flagged the rally as primarily short-covering, not genuine re-risking. ISM Prices Paid surged to 78.3, the strongest input-cost signal in months, currently masked by geopolitical optimism.
