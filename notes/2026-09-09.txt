The Treasury said Wednesday it would buy back only $6 billion of long-dated government debt — less than the $7–8 billion some traders expected. Rather than calming the bond market, that pushed the 10-year yield to 4.857%, a two-year high, and stocks fell within the next half hour. Oil kept climbing all afternoon while stocks recovered, so rates, not oil, drove the day. Small caps fell hardest (Russell 2000 −1.32%); chipmakers actually rose. Datadog and Marvell led the gainers on news already public days earlier — short covering, not new information. The real split: companies that must borrow to grow got sold.
