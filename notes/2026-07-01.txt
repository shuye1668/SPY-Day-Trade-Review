Bank of America's AI bubble-risk warning, colliding with NVIDIA's reported Vera Rubin memory reduction at the precise moment of half-year rebalancing, forced institutions to unwind the market's most crowded position after an 82% first-half semiconductor run. Liquidity migrated rather than exited: equipment and memory complexes de-rated as capital tilted toward laggard software, defensives, and financials. Simultaneously, collapsing ISM prices-paid and a war-premium unwind in oil hardened a disinflation regime, while ten-year yields backed up and punished duration-heavy growth. Breadth stayed positive; the S&P barely flinched. This is factor rotation, not systemic de-risking—forward posture hinges on hyperscaler capex and payrolls.
