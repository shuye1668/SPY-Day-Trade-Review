Hopes for a US-Iran ceasefire, landing the same day Dell reported blowout AI-server demand, pushed the S&P 500 to a ninth straight weekly gain. The two events removed Wall Street's biggest worries at once: war-driven oil inflation, and fears that AI spending was peaking. Oil slipped toward $92, bond yields eased, and money rushed out of defensive stocks and into AI hardware and software names. The headline indexes barely moved, but the action underneath was violent — winners soared while losers slid. Tellingly, small-cap stocks fell, showing the rally is narrow and concentrated. Next catalyst: June's Fed meeting.
