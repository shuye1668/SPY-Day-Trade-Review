A Wall Street Journal report exposing roughly $3 trillion of off-balance-sheet AI spending commitments across nine tech giants, landing alongside the 30-year Treasury yield hitting a 19-year high of 5.34%, triggered a sharp sell-off in the market's most crowded trade. Chipmakers led losses (SOX −4.98%), while the Dow barely moved (−0.22%). This was a rotation, not a panic: gold fell, defensive stocks rose, and the hardest-hit names (Coherent, Lumentum, Seagate) still have strong earnings. Money left AI hardware and moved into energy, healthcare, and non-AI software. The valuations were reset; the underlying demand hasn't broken yet.
