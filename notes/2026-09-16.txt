The Federal Reserve raised interest rates a quarter point to 3.75%–4%, but the hike was expected. Stocks turned lower when Chair Kevin Warsh said policy still isn't tight, signaling more hikes ahead. Two-year Treasury yields jumped to 4.71%, the dollar strengthened, and markets now see even odds of another hike in October. The Dow fell 1.2%, dragged by banks and truckers, while the S&P 500 slipped 0.45%. Chipmakers rose 0.6% as investors moved into AI hardware. Oil dropped nearly 5% intraday, yet stocks still fell, showing investors now fear the Fed more than oil. Key test ahead: whether 10-year yields break above 5.04%.
