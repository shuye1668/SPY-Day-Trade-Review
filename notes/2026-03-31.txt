The prospect of an end to the US-Iran war was the single most critical driver of Tuesday's dramatic risk-on reversal. The S&P 500 surged 2.91% and Nasdaq jumped 3.43%, fueled by signals from both Tehran and Washington suggesting willingness to de-escalate. Oil retreated from multi-year highs near $101, triggering a 20bp decline in the 10-year yield to ~4.28%. Semiconductors led gains (SOX +6.24%), amplified by Nvidia's $2B strategic investment in Marvell. Consumer confidence unexpectedly rose while JOLTS data showed orderly labor cooling.
