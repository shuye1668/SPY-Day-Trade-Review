SpaceX's record $75 billion IPO, oversubscribed twice over at $150 billion, pulled cash toward the upcoming deal just as Trump revived Iran-strike threats and traders braced for Wednesday's inflation report. To free up money, big investors sold their most crowded winners—the AI chip and software names that had run furthest. The S&P slipped 0.26%, but that flat number hides the real move: smaller companies, industrials, and defensive stocks rose as money rotated out of expensive tech into the broader economy. Tellingly, investors kept buying AI's physical hardware (APH, CARR) while dumping optics and software. Falling prices, rising price targets—repositioning, not collapse.
