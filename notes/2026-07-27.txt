Iran agreed to talks and held fire for a third day, sending Brent crude from $100 down to $88 and pulling bond yields lower. Cheaper oil and cheaper money lifted small caps, banks and most of the S&P 500. Chip stocks went the other way. Nvidia is now guaranteeing over $750 billion of deals so customers can afford its chips, and the cost of insuring its debt jumped the most on record — its bonds now yield more than Alphabet's. Apple, which spends little on AI, retook the crown as the world's most valuable company. Investors are sorting by who carries the risk, not who spends.
