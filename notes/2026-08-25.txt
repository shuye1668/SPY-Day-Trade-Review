Oil tumbled below $90 after signs that Middle East tensions were easing, which meant less worry about supply cutoffs. That hit two groups hard — oil producers and chemical makers — but it also cooled inflation fears, so investors bought bonds and pushed the 10-year yield down to 4.63%. Weak US data (shaky consumer confidence, sliding home sales) added to the bond buying. Lower yields make future profits worth more today, so money rotated into fast-growing tech, and chipmakers led ahead of Nvidia's earnings. Meanwhile a retailer's ugly forecast sank consumer stocks. The calm index hides a market betting everything on Nvidia.
