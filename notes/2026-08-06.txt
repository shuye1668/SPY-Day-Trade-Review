Oil jumped toward $83 after Iran moved to restrict shipping through the Strait of Hormuz, reviving fears that costlier energy keeps inflation sticky and pushes the Fed toward a rate hike. Bond yields and the dollar rose, which weighs on expensive tech stocks. Memory makers SanDisk and Western Digital beat estimates but gave cautious guidance, so their shares fell hard—yet Micron recovered by the close, showing the AI-memory boom is intact. Credit markets stayed calm, so this wasn't a scare. The drop was shallow and scattered—the Dow led losses while defense and energy earnings cushioned it. Friday's jobs report is the next test.
