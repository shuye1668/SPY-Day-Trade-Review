Oil broke above $90 just as the Fed held rates at 3.50–3.75% and stopped telling markets what comes next. With no guidance to lean on, investors dumped long-dated bonds — 30-year yields hit their highest since 2007 — while crowding into short maturities. Higher borrowing costs hurt the most expensive stocks worst, and chipmakers fell over 5%, because AI buildouts increasingly run on borrowed money: Oracle's cost of default insurance has nearly doubled this year. Banks fell too, on worries about who is funding all of it. Stocks closed at the day's low with nowhere to hide. Long-term yields now set the pace.
