August core inflation came in at 0.3%, a tenth hotter than forecast, and traders lifted the odds of a Fed rate hike this Wednesday to nearly 90%. Stocks still rose, because Brent crude fell back from $110 after Iran agreed to talk with Gulf states about reopening shipping through the Strait of Hormuz. Money chased the companies collecting Oracle's $90–95 billion data-center budget — HPE and Dell each jumped about 12% — while Oracle itself fell, its cost of credit insurance at a record high. That cash came out of this year's hottest memory stocks. Small caps barely moved; rising yields pulled prices off the high into the close.
