Meta's Muse assistant hit No. 1 on the app charts, and traders decided AI agents run on ordinary processors, not graphics chips. Money moved fast: CPU makers jumped and AMD passed $1 trillion, while oil, railroads and safe dividend stocks were sold to pay for it. Only falling oil helped — it pushed the 10-year yield under 5%. Short-term rates rose anyway, and default insurance on Oracle and other AI borrowers got more expensive. Few stocks joined the rally. It rests on an app ranking, not a confirmed chip order; the safer way to own it is the factories everyone must use.
