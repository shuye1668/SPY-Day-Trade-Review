Nvidia's forecast for roughly 70% revenue growth next year — capped by supply, not demand — sent its shares up nearly 9% and added $442 billion in value, the second-biggest one-day gain ever. Strong results from Salesforce and CrowdStrike lifted software too, easing fears that AI would eat the sector. But this wasn't a broad rally: chips and tech soared while most other stocks fell, as money left retail, travel and defensive names to chase AI winners. Bonds, the dollar and volatility barely moved, so the shift was orderly. All eyes now turn to Fed Chair Warsh's Friday speech.
