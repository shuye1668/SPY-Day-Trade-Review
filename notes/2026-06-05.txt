A hotter-than-expected May payrolls print (+172k vs ~85k consensus) collided with Broadcom's decision to merely reiterate—not raise—its AI chip guidance, simultaneously repricing the risk-free rate higher and cracking the "AI capex grows forever" narrative. Institutions executed dual de-risking: shedding long-duration semiconductors while migrating liquidity into low-beta staples, insurers, and healthcare. The tell was defensives rallying against rising yields (UNH/KO/PG up as 10Y hit 4.55%)—proof this was positioning unwind, not a rate trade. The result is a barbell posture: chips collapsed (SOX -8.7%) while blue-chips held (INDU -1.2%). Forward risk lies not in AI, but in a second discount-rate shock should hiring stay strong.
