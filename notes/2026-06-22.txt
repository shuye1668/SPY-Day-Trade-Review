Two of Alphabet's top AI scientists left for rivals Anthropic and OpenAI the same day SpaceX's stock tumbled 16% while borrowing $20 billion for computing. The pairing sharpened one worry: the tech giants may be spending on AI faster than it pays off. Ten-year Treasury yields climbing toward 4.51% deepened the pain for pricey software and consumer stocks. Investors didn't abandon AI—they rotated down the supply chain, from cash-burning platforms into the hardware profiting from the boom: memory, optics, power, and equipment. The split was stark—the Dow and chipmakers rose while big-tech indexes fell. What follows hinges on whether that spending finally delivers returns.
