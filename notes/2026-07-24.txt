Oil fell back below $100 after Pakistan began exploring a way to restart US–Iran talks and tankers kept moving through the Middle East. That removed the inflation scare that hit stocks a day earlier — yet the S&P 500 finished flat and chip stocks fell 4.3%. The reason showed up in the bond market: investors gave a lukewarm reception to a BlackRock-arranged bond funding a Meta data center. If lenders won't finance AI buildouts, the companies selling the parts lose orders. Memory, optics and AI cloud stocks were dumped; software, Apple and data-center landlords were bought. Megacap earnings and the Fed decide next.
