A washed-out AI-hardware complex found aggressive buyers as two tail risks faded at once—an Iran de-escalation and a Supreme Court ruling affirming Fed independence—compressing the duration risk premium on dollar assets. Capital rotated back into the most beaten-down long-duration growth: semiconductor-capex and memory names led, funded by the sale of defensive staples, materials and rate-sensitive telecom. Crucially, this was a narrow factor re-risking, not broad reflation—small-caps finished flat, and the rebound overcame declines in the two largest index weights. The rally's durability now hinges on hyperscaler capex guidance holding; breadth, not direction, is the binding constraint.
