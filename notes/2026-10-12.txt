A Chinese memory maker’s claim of a denser chip design gave investors a reason to cash out of the most crowded AI hardware trades. Yet the selling ranked by how far stocks had run, not by who the news hurts: optical names with no exposure fell about 10%, more than Micron. The money stayed invested, moving into energy, staples and megacaps such as Meta, so the S&P 500 lost only four points on the year’s thinnest volume. That calm rests on a narrow base. Bank earnings, Wednesday’s inflation data and TSMC will decide whether rotation holds or the chip slide spreads.
