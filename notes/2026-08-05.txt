A tentative Hormuz shipping deal sank crude roughly 6%, pulling the 10-year yield down to 4.61% and handing importers a clear break on energy costs. Yet Wall Street paused after a four-day, $3.7-trillion sprint: the Dow closed at a record while chips fell 1.4%, as even blowout earnings from Western Digital, Astera Labs and AMD were sold. This was profit-taking at the top, not a broken story — SanDisk's record margins confirmed the memory upcycle is intact, and bank credit stayed calm. Nvidia bucked the fade on a SpaceX win. Friday's jobs report now decides whether the rate relief holds.
