June inflation came in cooler than expected—consumer prices actually fell for the first time in six years—and that single number reset the day. Traders quickly abandoned bets on a July Fed rate hike, sending both stocks and bonds higher, with short-term Treasury yields dropping the most. Money rotated into beaten-down chip stocks: semiconductors jumped 2.5%, Nvidia led, and the Nasdaq gained 1.1% while the Dow barely moved. Strong earnings from JPMorgan and Goldman added fuel, though Citi lagged. One caveat: oil rebounded on Persian Gulf tensions, so inflation relief is real—but not yet a full all-clear.
