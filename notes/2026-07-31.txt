Apple warned it can't get enough memory chips and advanced chipmaking capacity — shortages created by the AI data-center boom — and cut its sales outlook. The stock fell 7.35% on Friday, its worst day since March 2020. Money moved instead to companies that own the scarce supply: Amazon jumped 15.32%, Alphabet 6.73%, Microsoft 3.02%. The S&P 500 rose 0.70%, but chipmakers were flat and small caps fell. Hot wage and inflation data pushed the 5-year Treasury yield up 17 basis points on the week. Then over the weekend Trump halted strikes on Iran, and Brent crude crashed 7.7%.
