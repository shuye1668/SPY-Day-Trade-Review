Cisco's earnings blew past expectations, but the real shock was its AI order book: full-year guidance for hyperscaler orders jumped from $5B to $9B. That single, credible number — actual orders, not a spending forecast — convinced investors that AI infrastructure spending runs far deeper than chips alone. Money rotated hard into networking, custom silicon, and AI-adjacent industrials, while non-AI sectors like commercial real estate and bank software were sold despite no bad news of their own. The S&P topped 7,500 for the first time. The catch: indexes hit records on narrow leadership, leaving the rally fragile if catalysts dry up.
