US-Iran fighting flared overnight, sending oil to nearly $96 and reigniting inflation worries just as strong jobs and services data hit. Traders flipped from betting on Fed rate cuts to pricing a possible hike this year — a view Dallas Fed chief Logan openly floated — pushing 10-year Treasury yields toward 4.5%. That snapped the S&P 500's nine-day winning streak. Pricey software and crypto-linked stocks fell hardest, while money rotated into energy, healthcare, and staples like Walmart. Memory-chip makers rallied on their own shortage story, keeping semiconductors green. Next up: ceasefire talks and Friday's payrolls report.
