A leaked Meta memo revealing plans to double its computing power to 14GW next year—plus SK Hynix's US listing drawing seven times more orders than shares available—reignited the AI hardware trade just as doubts about overbuilding had cooled it. Money flooded back into chip, memory, and optical stocks, though notably Nvidia fell, showing investors rotating within the AI theme rather than adding fresh bets. Oil dropped 2% as Iran-related fears faded, hurting energy shares, while rising rate expectations pushed investors out of dividend-heavy defensive names. Next test: earnings season must now prove the spending pays off.
