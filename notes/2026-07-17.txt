A Chinese startup, Moonshot, showed its new open-source model Kimi K3 designing a full chip in 48 hours using free software — raising doubts about how protected chip-design companies really are. At the same time, US-Iran strikes pushed oil up nearly 12% for the week. Higher oil means stickier inflation, so bond yields refused to fall even as stocks dropped. Investors didn't flee the market; they moved money out of expensive AI-linked names and into businesses earning cash today — insurers, refiners, health plans. Credit markets stayed calm throughout. This was a rotation between sectors, not the start of a crash.
