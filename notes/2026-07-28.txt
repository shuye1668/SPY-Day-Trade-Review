Brent fell to $84, its worst three days since 2020, as Middle East tensions cooled. That pushed bond yields down for a third straight day and calmed inflation fears, lifting the pressure that had held stocks back since mid-July. But the relief money did not go back where it came from. It went into companies posting solid profits and into ordinary economic names, sending the equal-weighted S&P 500 to a record and the Dow up 1% (Coca-Cola, Boeing). AI hardware fell alone, on track for its worst month since 2002. Bond markets disagreed: the cost of insuring AI borrowers' debt kept rising.
