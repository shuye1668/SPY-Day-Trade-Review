Oil dropping below $80 — on hopes a looming US-Iran peace deal will restore supply — pulled bond yields lower and set off a sharp rotation, one day before new Fed chair Kevin Warsh's first rate decision. Investors took profits in red-hot AI chips and shifted into banks and steadier value stocks. Chipmakers slid (SOX -5.71%; Marvell, Intel, AMD down 7–10% after NVIDIA unveiled rival PC chips), dragging the S&P 500 -0.57% and Nasdaq 100 about 2% lower. Yet the Dow hit a record (+0.64%), led by JPMorgan. The key point: this was profit-taking after a big run, not an AI collapse.
