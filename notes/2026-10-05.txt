Europe’s political and fiscal turmoil pushed long-term borrowing costs higher worldwide: the euro hit a 17-month low and US 30-year yields reached multi-decade highs, with the 10-year at 5.31%. A hot prices gauge in the ISM services survey added to the pressure, yet stocks rose as the same report showed hiring and orders holding up. Money crowded into cash-rich AI giants that can fund growth without borrowing—Nvidia hit a record, lifting the Nasdaq 100 to a new peak—while rate-sensitive homebuilders and debt-funded AI cloud renters lagged. The rally is real but narrow; a 10-year yield above 5.35% is the line to watch.
