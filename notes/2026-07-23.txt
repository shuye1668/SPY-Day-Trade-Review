Brent's break above $100 on an intensifying US–Iran conflict collided with an AI-spending credibility test, as Alphabet's raised capital-expenditure guide triggered the worst megacap session since the April 2025 tariff shock. The twin blow—an energy-inflation impulse lifting real yields to fresh year-highs and doubt over hyperscaler returns—converged on one factor: long-duration growth was de-risked while Fed-hike odds repriced from 10% to 35%. Capital rotated into cash-generative defensives, with defense and quality healthcare (LMT, RTX) absorbing the flow; chipmakers outran their own customers, cushioned by the memory upcycle. Posture stays defensive, hinging now on hyperscaler earnings validating spend and the July 29 Fed.
