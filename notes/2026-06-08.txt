Chips came roaring back Monday after Friday's brutal $1 trillion selloff. The stocks that fell hardest—memory makers, chip-equipment names, and foundries like Intel—bounced the most, helped by short-covering and easing Middle East tensions as Iran wound down its strikes. But the rebound was more relief than recovery: bond yields stayed stubbornly high near 4.57% after Friday's strong jobs report killed hopes for rate cuts. Money rotated out of defensive names, drugmakers, and rate-sensitive REITs back into AI plays. Two standouts had real news behind them—Intel landed a giant Google chip deal, and Marvell won S&P 500 inclusion.
