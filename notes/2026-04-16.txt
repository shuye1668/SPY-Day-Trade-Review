US equities eked out fresh record highs as Trump signaled progress on an Iran deal and announced a 10-day Israel-Lebanon ceasefire, easing Strait of Hormuz supply concerns. The S&P 500 rose 0.26% to 7,041; Nasdaq 100 added 0.49%; SOX outperformed at +0.97% on TSMC's 58% profit surge. Jobless claims at 207k and Philly Fed at 26.7 reinforced economic resilience, though industrial production fell 0.5%. The 10-year yield climbed to 4.31% as haven demand faded. Brent slipped to $98.15. Paulson warned on Treasury demand risks while IMF delegates cautioned markets underprice war damage. Internal rotation shows AI chip design leading, equipment names lagging.
