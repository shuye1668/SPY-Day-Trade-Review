An energy-led CPI spike to 4.2%—amplified by Iran-driven oil and a soft 10-year auction—drove Treasury yields to 4.55% and pushed markets to price a December Fed hike. Higher real yields detonated the year's most crowded trade, semiconductors, forcing duration de-risking out of long-duration growth. Compounding it, looming mega-cap equity supply—SpaceX's IPO, SMCI's $7B raise—pressured managers to trim winners for cash. Capital migrated into energy, staples, and bond-proxy defensives. Crucially, breadth stayed positive even as the cap-weighted index fell: a leadership rotation, not a liquidation. Oil and yields now dictate whether tech stabilizes or the value rotation extends.
