Nvidia's Jensen Huang used his Computex keynote to knock down fears that AI will kill software companies — and unveiled a new chip pushing into Intel's PC turf. That lit a fire under beaten-down software and AI-chip stocks, sending the S&P 500 to an eighth straight record. But the rally was narrow: investors sold mega-caps like Meta and Amazon to fund it, small caps fell, and a strong factory report pushed bond yields higher, raising talk the Fed's next move could be a hike. Oil rose despite Middle East de-escalation. A record on the surface, fragile underneath.
